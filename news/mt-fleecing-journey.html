The Fleecing Journey of MT: 13 Trades, 4 Seasons, and One DM You Should Not Open

Every league has one. The guy whose name pops up in your notifications at 11:48 pm on a Tuesday with "you up? 👀 got a 2-for-1 for you." In Echo Frezco, that guy is MT (Yahoo handle mark), now running Love Thy Nabers, previously the CaTTal1na franchise in its various spellings.

League lore says MT has sent an offer to every manager in this league at least once a week since roughly the invention of the forward pass. League lore also says the Yahoo trade screen has a "Decline all from mark" button that only you can see. We can't verify either. What we can verify is the 13 trades that actually went through from 2023 to 2026, and what every player did afterward.

How we scored it: for every player in every trade, we added up his full-PPR points (Sleeper's weekly stats) from the first NFL week whose Sunday came after the trade, through Week 17. Picks are listed by Yahoo round only. We have no idea what they turned into, so we don't pretend to.

Fleece meter: 🐑🐑🐑🐑🐑 = MT sheared you bald. 🐑 = MT walked away cold. ✂️ = MT got fleeced.

2023: CaTTal1na, 7-7, finished 3rd

The origin story. A 6 seed who won the 3rd-place game 121.88-100.60, and a man learning his craft.

Sept 22 vs. The vanilla gorilla (Dylan): MT sent Gus Edwards + a Round 16 pick for Luke Musgrave + a Round 3 pick. After the trade: Edwards 166.80 in 14 games. Musgrave 60.60 in 8. On the field, MT lost by 106.20 points. On the draft board, he turned a 16th into a 3rd. Classic MT: lose the battle, steal the pick. Fleece meter: 🐑🐑

Oct 13 vs. Deebo on the trade block (Chris): MT sent Miles Sanders + Dallas Goedert for T.J. Hockenson. After the trade: Sanders 41.70 + Goedert 87.40 = 129.10. Hockenson alone: 153.60 in 10 games. The textbook 2-for-1: give a man two players, take the best one, and outscore both of them by 24.50. Chris's team name was "Deebo on the trade block," which in hindsight was a cry for help. Fleece meter: 🐑🐑🐑🐑

Nov 11 vs. E.T. Phone Mahomes (Sean): MT sent Jaylen Warren + Round 6 + Round 15 for a Round 4 and a Round 5 (both originally vanilla gorilla's). After the trade: Warren 106.20 in 8 games. MT got 0.00 points and two better picks. Remember the name Jaylen Warren. MT certainly did. Fleece meter: 🐑🐑

The final tally

Across the 11 trades from 2023-2025, the players MT received scored 410.20 fewer post-trade points than the players he gave away. He "won" only 3 of those 11 on the field: Hockenson, Charbonnet, and Adams. So is the fleece fake?

No. Look at what he kept getting back: a 3rd for a 16th (twice), a 2nd for a 10th, a 1st for a 4th, a 4th and 5th for a 6th and 15th. He keeps selling points for pick upgrades and still finished 3rd, 5th and 2nd. The fleece isn't in the box score. It's in the draft board and the standings, and in the fact that people keep saying yes.

Best fleece: a 2024 Round 3 pick for Zach Charbonnet, who then scored 82.60 points in 7 games. Worst fleece (against MT): pick a lane. Caleb Williams + a 3rd for Sutton + a 15th lost on both fronts, and the Adonai Mitchell deal cost him a 1st for a 3rd and a 5th.

Lock your DMs

Love Thy Nabers is 3-2, 5th place, with 728.02 PF and the league's lowest points against (578.40). Two trades in five days with the same 0-5 team. The trade deadline is November 28. That's about six more weeks of 11:48 pm "you up?" offers.

So: mute the notifications. Turn off trade alerts. If a message from mark starts with "hear me out," put the phone down and walk into the ocean. And if you do accept, at least ask for his 1st-rounder. Historically, it's the one thing he'll give back.
