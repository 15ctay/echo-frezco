Tank You Very Much: Dylan's Four-Year Quest for a First-Round Pick He Can Trade Away

League lore says that every year, somewhere around Week 6, Dylan looks at his roster, sighs, and starts losing on purpose for a better draft pick. We can't confirm what happens in his heart. We can confirm what happens on Yahoo, and it's somehow funnier.

Because here's the twist: Dylan tanks for picks, then trades the picks away. When he does keep a pick, he trades the player he drafted with it. Sometimes he trades the same player twice. The tank isn't a strategy. It's a lifestyle.

The many names of the tank

Every great rebuild needs a rebrand.

  • 2023-2024: The vanilla gorilla. The classic.
  • 2025: Dlow is king. A bold claim for a 4-10 team.
  • 2026: King of Onlyfannins 🤴🤴. Two crowns, zero first-round picks.

2023: The vanilla gorilla, 6-8, 7th

Season one of our story starts like an anti-tank, frankly. Dylan had three first-rounders in that draft: 1.03 Jordan Addison, 1.07 Khalil Herbert, 1.09 Dalvin Cook. On Sept 19 he traded a Round 1, Round 4 and Round 5 to E.T. Phone Mahomes for Joe Mixon and three late picks (R14, R15, R16). That's a man buying veterans in September.

Result: 6-8, 7th, 1,782.02 points for (9th). And for the 2024 draft? No Round 1 picks. All traded away. Tanking requires a pick at the end of it. He had already spent them.

Tank-o-meter: 🛢️ (the tank hadn't been invented yet)

2024: The vanilla gorilla, 2-12, dead last

This is the masterpiece. 2-12. Last place. The league's lowest points for (1,677.20). He finished on an 8-game losing streak, and nobody can say he didn't commit.

The trades were busy:

  • Oct 15: Sent DK Metcalf + R15 + R16 to Sweet Sperm Martinez for Jordan Addison + R1 + R2. Yes, the same Jordan Addison he took 1.03 a year earlier. Draft him, lose him, buy him back.
  • Oct 22: Sent Ricky Pearsall + two Round 1s (his own and the one he got a week earlier) to Play With My Hock? for Tyreek Hill + R14 + R15. A team on its way to 2-12 trading two firsts for a veteran receiver. Bold.
  • Nov 14, the fire sale: three trades in one day.
  • Mike Evans + Travis Kelce + R4 to JO for Travis Etienne Jr., Kyle Pitts Sr. and an R1 (from Drizzy University).
  • Joe Mixon + R14 + R15 to Sweet Sperm Martinez for Rhamondre Stevenson, Jakobi Meyers, R3, R4.
  • Tyreek Hill (three weeks after paying two 1sts for him) + R13 + R14 to Chicken Nico for Keon Coleman, an R1 and an R2.

Evans, Kelce, Mixon and Hill went out the door. Youth and two first-rounders came back. Out of context, a real rebuild. In context, he'd just traded two first-rounders for Hill and then flipped Hill for one first-rounder 23 days later. The tank ate itself.

Tank-o-meter: 🛢️🛢️🛢️🛢️🛢️ (peak tank)

2025: Dlow is king, 4-10, 9th

The payoff. The 2025 draft opened with 1.03 TreVeyon Henderson and 1.05 Emeka Egbuka, most likely the picks he gathered in those 2024 deals. Two top-five picks! The rebuild was finally here!

And then, in October:

  • Oct 11: Egbuka + R3 + R16 to Indiana Jones for DK Metcalf + Nico Collins + R2 + R4. Yes, DK Metcalf again, the one he traded away a year earlier. The man doesn't rebuild. He re-collects.
  • Oct 15: Henderson + Jordan Love + R13 + R14 to YAC & cheese for Kendre Miller, an R1 and an R3.
  • Oct 19: Nick Chubb + that new R1 to CaTTel1na (yes, MT) for Kenneth Walker III, Zach Charbonnet and an R4.

So both 2025 first-round picks were gone by Week 7. Plus the replacement first-rounder, four days after he got it. The 2026 draft: no Round 1 pick for King of Onlyfannins. Traded away.

He also ran a small pick-flipping loop in two days: sent Kyle Pitts Sr. + R12 for an R4 (Oct 18), then sent that R4 for Luther Burden III + R16 (Oct 19). Kyle Pitts Sr. became Luther Burden III in a single day, the fantasy equivalent of a garage sale.

Also in the 2025 log: commissioner ABM changed his Week 4 lineup (Sept 28). League lore says this is the commish's way of checking for a pulse. We'd like to clarify that nobody has proved anything about anyone's lineups, and we are absolutely not accusing a 4-10 man of not trying. We're accusing him of trying too hard at the wrong thing.

Final: 4-10, 9th, 1,545.08 points for (11th). Then the bracket gave him a lower-placement game, which Dlow is king won 82.30-70.04 over Indiana Jones. Even his tank has a winning streak.

Tank-o-meter: 🛢️🛢️🛢️ (tanked the season, also traded the reason for tanking)

The fine print nobody read

Here's the part that should hurt the most. We checked the league settings for 2025 and 2026: there's no toilet bowl or consolation rule that sets draft order. Draft type is Live Standard, with keepers on and pick trading allowed. Nothing in Settings ties finishing last to picking first.

So does tanking even help in this league? League lore says yes. The settings say "citation needed." Either way, it doesn't matter, because Dylan isn't keeping the pick.

2026: King of Onlyfannins 🤴🤴, 3-2, 7th

The draft: no first-rounder (again). His first pick was 2.04 Blake Corum, followed by 2.11 Josh Downs, 2.12 Michael Pittman Jr., 3.01 Kenny Gainwell and 3.06 Wan'Dale Robinson. Then, in Round 6 (#72): Emeka Egbuka. The man re-drafted his own traded first-rounder, which is either redemption or a cry for help.

Then, Sept 29: Egbuka + an R1 to Goldie for Jaylen Waddle, Tetairoa McMillan and an R16. He traded Emeka Egbuka for the second year in a row, and another first-round pick went with him. On Oct 7 he sent an R2 and an R4 to BK The King for David Montgomery and two late picks. Montgomery is now on IR with an O tag.

A hopeful note, sort of

Credit where it's due. A 3-2 record with the league's 10th-best scoring takes a special kind of talent, or a special kind of luck. If this squad sneaks into the 6-team playoff, Dylan will have finally found the secret: you can't trade away a playoff spot.

He'll probably try, though. Somebody check whether "R1 2027" is still in his trophy case.
