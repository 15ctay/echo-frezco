Every Trade, Graded: 52 Deals, 4 Seasons, and the Tank Commander Is the Best Trader in the League

We went back through every trade in the Echo Frezco Keeper League since 2023: 52 trades (12 in 2023, 16 in 2024, 19 in 2025, 5 so far in 2026). For each one we scored what each side got: the points its new players put up for their new team for the rest of that season. Then we added it all up by manager.

The results are not what anyone would have predicted. The guy famous for tanking is the best trader in the league. The commissioner is the worst. The defending champions have never won a trade.

How it's scored. Sleeper full-PPR points (pts_ppr), weeks after the trade through Week 17. If a player got traded again the same season, his points stop counting for that team at the next trade. Players later dropped to waivers are not tracked. Draft picks score 0.00. (Every trade in this league swaps the same number of picks each way, so the table tracks 1st-rounders, the only picks that really move.) We don't credit picks with whoever ended up being drafted with them, because the trade logs don't track picks forward. So pick buyers look worse here than they may deserve, and pick sellers look better. Net trade points = points received minus points given, summed over every trade. A trade's winner is the side whose new players scored more. A push is 0.00 to 0.00. 2026 counts through Week 6, still in progress (stats pulled ~6:45 PM ET Sunday). Ronald ran Stroud & Proud alone in 2023, listed here as Ronald (solo era). ctay joined as co-manager in 2024, and from then on the team is the Co-Men (1 Team 2 Throats in 2024, Brock Tuah in 2025-26).
