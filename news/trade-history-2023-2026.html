Every Trade, Graded: 52 Deals, 4 Seasons, and the Tank Commander Is the Best Trader in the League

We went back through every trade in the Echo Frezco Keeper League since 2023: 52 trades (12 in 2023, 16 in 2024, 19 in 2025, 5 so far in 2026). Every trade gets scored two ways: what each side's new players scored, and what the draft picks it got were worth. Add them up, and you get a winner. Then we added it all up by manager.

The guy famous for tanking is the best trader in the league. Almost all of that came off one man.

How it's scored

Player points: Sleeper full-PPR (pts_ppr) points each received player scored for his new team, from the week after the trade through Week 17. If the player got traded again that season, his points stop at the next trade. Later waiver drops are not tracked. 2026 counts through Week 6, still in progress (pulled ~6:45 PM ET Sunday).
Pick points: each received draft pick counts by round. R1 150 · R2 120 · R3 100 · R4 80 · R5 65 · R6 50 · R7 40 · R8 30 · R9 25 · R10 20 · R11 15 · R12 12 · R13 9 · R14 6 · R15 4 · R16 2. Picks in add value, picks out subtract it.
Why a fixed scale: the box only has the 2026 Echo draft, not 2023-2025. And in this keeper league, real draft results run backwards: kept stars get slotted into late rounds. Through five weeks of 2026, Round 16 picks are on pace for 285.9 full-PPR points and Round 1 for 162.2, so averaging by round would make a 16th worth more than a 1st. So we use a plain descending scale. Round 1 = 150 is roughly the 2026 Round 1 class's 17-game pace (162.2), and the value roughly halves every three to four rounds. Every trade here swaps the same number of picks each way, so pick value is all about which rounds change hands.
Totals: a trade's winner is the side with more players + picks. Total net = everything received minus everything given, summed over every trade, which nets to exactly 0.00 across the league. Ronald and ctay co-manage one team, listed as the Co-Men: Stroud & Proud in 2023, 1 Team 2 Throats in 2024, Brock Tuah in 2025-26.
