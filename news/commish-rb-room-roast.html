Every league needs a commissioner. Someone to approve trades, settle disputes and quietly wonder why his team keeps scoring 39 points below projection. In Echo Frezco that man is ABM, manager of Bijan in the Nico Time. We audited his running back room using the Oct 27 Yahoo data. We say this with love: the man is running a two-man show with three understudies who forgot their lines.

Even The Athletic had notes

We went looking for the Athletic piece that supposedly named the commish's whole team. We couldn't find a single recent article covering his entire roster, so we won't pretend one exists. What we did find is The Athletic covering his running backs one at a time this summer, and it's arguably worse:

  • Aug. 13: Do 'whatever it takes' to get Jayden Daniels, Omarion Hampton in fantasy football 2026. Hampton was billed as a "likely sophomore sensation" who "possesses fiery fingers." ABM did whatever it took. The fingers have produced 57.50 points in six games.
  • Aug. 24: Top targets and fades for the first 8 rounds. Listed under "Fade: TreVeyon Henderson." His upside was "contingent on Rhamondre Stevenson going down." Stevenson is still standing. He's on the Co-Men's roster, which is a separate tragedy.
  • Aug. 26: The top 5 players to sell right now in dynasty league fantasy football. Henderson again, tagged "Priority sell if you are… any team." Any team. That includes teams run by commissioners.
  • Aug. 28: The "lover's" top 50 had Bijan at No. 2 and McCaffrey at No. 5. To be fair, those two are delivering. The Athletic's optimist and ABM agree on exactly two running backs.

So The Athletic told the league to chase Hampton, fade Henderson and sell Henderson, and the commissioner did the first one and ignored the other two.

The trade: buying back your own pick, the hard way

ABM hasn't made a trade in 2026. That makes his most recent one the October 2025 Luther Burden III saga, which has to be told in order:

  1. Oct 4, 2025: ABM gets Luther Burden III + a Round 2 from Joshua for Troy Franklin + a Round 5. Our trade calculator: Joshua by 74.9.
  2. Oct 16: ABM sends his own Round 4 to Adrian for Rachaad White + a Round 16. ABM by 68.0. Credit where it's due.
  3. Oct 18: Adrian flips that same Round 4 to Dylan, as part of the Kyle Pitts deal.
  4. Oct 19: ABM sends Luther Burden III + a Round 16 to Dylan to get back... "Round 4 (traded from Gen Z-Bo & Co. \\\*)." His own pick, three days after trading it away.

Burden scored 84.6 for Dylan the rest of the way. Final: Dylan by 84.6. Count it up: the commish turned Troy Franklin into Burden, Burden into the pick he already owned, and lost both trades doing it. It's the fantasy version of selling your car and then paying the buyer extra to get it back.

The career picture, from our trade history: 14 trades, 3-11, -215.80 net. That's 11 trade losses, the most in the league.

And the most recent trade he touched? As commissioner he approved the Oct 21 deal that sent Alvin Kamara + Jordan Mason to King of Onlyfannins for Josh Downs + Kendrick Bourne, going to The Man in Jahmyrror. That's Joshua, his Week 8 opponent. Kamara and Mason scored 20.0 in Week 7 and Downs and Bourne 7.7, so on paper the commish waved through a trade that made his own opponent worse. Yahoo still has Joshua by 23.44 this week (140.64 to 117.20). It's the most generous approval in league history, and it didn't help him at all.
