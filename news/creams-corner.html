Five combos for degenerates

2. The Foxborough Family Plan: Hunter Henry + Rhamondre Stevenson: +379 ($47.87). Two Patriots, one red zone. Also a co-parenting arrangement between Love Thy Nabers and Brock Tuah, which neither of them agreed to.
