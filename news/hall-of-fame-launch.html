The Hall of Fame is open

The site has two new pages in the nav:

  • Hall of Fame: champion banners, all-time standings, a record book, and the Echo Frezco Mount Rushmore.
  • Head-to-Head: every manager against every manager since 2023, regular season and playoffs. Pick a manager and see your record, points for and against, streaks and last meeting against everyone.

Everything is by manager, not team name, because this league changes team names more often than it sets lineups. Ronald & ctay are counted as one manager, The Co-Men, across Stroud & Proud (2023), 1 Team 2 Throats (2024) and Brock Tuah (2025-26). Numbers run from 2023 through 2026 Week 6. Who's your daddy? A paternity investigation

Anthony (Two Browns in the LaPorta Pot, now OnlyFannins) and Joshua (JO, then YAC & 🧀, now The Man in Jahmyrror) have long been suspected of being father and son. We were asked to decide who's the father. We asked the head-to-head data. The results are in.

Joshua is the father. He's 3-1 against Anthony all-time:

SeasonResult
2023 Week 11Joshua 122.50, Anthony 98.44. First lesson.

2026 Week 4Anthony 130.02, Joshua 128.88. The first rebellion, by 1.14.

Every family has the year the kid gets a car and thinks he's grown. For Anthony it's 2026. OnlyFannins is 5-1 and first in the standings, and The Man in Jahmyrror is 4-2 and fourth. The son is ahead of his father for the first time, and he's going to bring it up at Thanksgiving.

Anthony has had his moments. He finished 2nd in 2024 while Dad was 3rd, and that 221.42 is the best playoff score in league history. But he lost that final to Brian, and the series is still 3-1. Anthony, until you win the rematch, you're still on Joshua's phone plan.
