The Hall of Fame is open

The site has two new pages in the nav:

  • Hall of Fame: champion banners, all-time standings, a record book, and the Echo Frezco Mount Rushmore.

Who's your daddy? A paternity investigation

Anthony (Two Browns in the LaPorta Pot, now OnlyFannins) and Joshua (JO, then YAC & 🧀, now The Man in Jahmyrror) have long been suspected of being father and son. We were asked to decide who's the father. We asked the head-to-head data. The results are in.

Joshua is the father. With the 2018-2022 archive in, he's 5-4 against Anthony all-time and has outscored him 1,207.52 to 1,105.30. It's closer than the family photos suggest:

So it's not the son's first rebellion. It's his second. The first was 2018-19, when JuJu on that TD beat JO three straight times, including the only playoff meeting, and then went 12-1. Dad answered with four straight wins over six years.

Anthony has the better résumé: a .667 win % to Joshua's .557, three runner-ups, the 13-game streak, and OnlyFannins is 5-1 and first right now while The Man in Jahmyrror is 4-2 and fourth. But Dad has the most wins in league history (64), the most points (15,266.82) and the series lead, 5-4. Anthony, tie it up and we'll talk. Until then you're still on Joshua's phone plan.
