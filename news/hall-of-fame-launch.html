Now Open: The Echo Frezco Hall of Fame, a Mount Rushmore, and Head-to-Head (Plus Proof Joshua Is Anthony's Dad)

The Hall of Fame is open

The site has two new pages in the nav:

  • Hall of Fame: champion banners, all-time standings, a record book, and the Echo Frezco Mount Rushmore.
  • Head-to-Head: every manager against every manager since 2018, regular season and playoffs. Pick a manager and see your record, points for and against, streaks and last meeting against everyone.

Everything is by manager, not team name, because this league changes team names more often than it sets lineups. Ronald & ctay are counted as one manager, The Co-Men, across Stroud & Proud (2023), 1 Team 2 Throats (2024) and Brock Tuah (2025-26).

Now with 2018-2022, with names. Yahoo hides every manager for 2018-2022, so Corey went through the old teams and matched them by nickname: MT = mark, BK = Brian, JO = Joshua, AB = ABMCommissioner, AD = Anthony, Teep = Antonio, Tribe = Chris, BJ = Benedetto, Dlow = Dylan, and Skeet = Ronnie (Ronald). Ronald's solo years (2018-2022) are part of the Co-Men row, and the page says they were solo. BC (2 girls one Kupp, Legends Return) is a past manager with no current team. One snag: two 2021 teams, Lamar Morghulis and Armed J. Robbery, were both tagged as Anthony, so both stay under their team names until that's sorted out. Numbers run from 2018 through 2026 Week 6.

Mount Rushmore

Ranked by titles, then playoff trips, then playoff wins, then wins, then points.

  1. ABMCommissioner: three titles (Cook Crack Engrams 2019, Robby 2 Hottie 2020, LaMartinez 2023), 7-2 in the playoffs, 60-41 all-time, and never last. He won finals against Anthony (2019) and Ronald (2020). He runs the league, and he wins it.
  2. mark: 2022 champion (CaTTalina W1ne M1xon, 125.16-121.50 over Ronald), runner-up in 2021 and 2025, and the most playoff trips (7). Also finished last in 2020.
  3. The Co-Men (Ronald & ctay): 2025 champions, plus Ronald's solo runner-ups in 2020 and 2022. 65-50 regular season, 5-4 playoffs, 70-54 overall, and the most points in league history (16,205.70). Ronald lost two finals and finished last in 2021 in between. Corey joined, and they won a title. The desk is just presenting the timeline.
  4. Brian: 2024 champion (BK The King, 173.98-160.66 over Anthony), 3rd in 2018, 57-58 all-time. He beat Dylan for the last spot on wins.

Just missed: Dylan (2018 champion), Sean (2021 champion with DK Country), Anthony and Joshua.

Who's your daddy? A paternity investigation

Anthony (Two Browns in the LaPorta Pot, now OnlyFannins) and Joshua (JO, then YAC & 🧀, now The Man in Jahmyrror) have long been suspected of being father and son. We were asked to decide who's the father. We asked the head-to-head data. The results are in.

Joshua is the father. With the full archive, he's 6-4 against Anthony and has outscored him 1,309.04 to 1,205.52:

That's the son's second rebellion. The first was 2018-19, when JuJu on that TD beat JO three straight times, including their only playoff meeting. Dad answered with five straight.

On paper Anthony is better: the most wins in league history (68), a .673 win % to Joshua's .557, three runner-ups, and OnlyFannins is 5-1 and first right now while The Man in Jahmyrror is 4-2 and fourth. But Anthony is 0-3 in finals, and Dad leads the series 6-4. Anthony, win a ring or tie the series and we'll talk. Until then you're still on Joshua's phone plan.
