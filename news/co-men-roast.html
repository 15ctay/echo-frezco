The Co-Men: Two Grown Men Have Run Brock Tuah Since 2024, and They're Still Losing by 0.22

Every team in the Echo Frezco Keeper League has a manager. Eleven of them get by with one. Brock Tuah has needed two since 2024: Ronald and ctay. They're the only team in the league sharing a login: one roster, two brains, and no evidence either brain is in charge.

We call them the Co-Men. This is year three of the experiment. Let's see what two heads have accomplished.

2024: Two managers, zero trophies

The Co-Men era started in 2024. Two managers, a full season, every waiver wire and trade window doubled up. The 2024 title went to BK The King (Brian). Brian did it alone. The Co-Men had twice the staff and got the same trophy as Dylan, who went 2-12: none.

The 2025 "title"

Brock Tuah are the defending champions. They beat MT's CaTTel1na 147.72-97.20 in the 2025 final, and both of them will tell you about it. Fine. Both of them can own it. Let's look at what they own.

They won a championship game against a team that scored 97.20. In a final. In 2025 alone, MT's trades gave away 244.60 more post-trade points than they brought back (we covered this). He then showed up to the final with 97 points, and the Co-Men were handed a trophy for beating a guy who'd spent the season trading points for picks. That's not a title run. That's winning a race against someone who stopped halfway to sell his shoes, and needing two people to carry the medal.

The funniest part: MT made 13 trades over four seasons, and Brock Tuah isn't in a single one of them. The one team that never took his call won the title he spent all year funding. Two managers, and the championship strategy was "let MT trade with everyone else."

And the record keepers? The 2025 championship is listed as "Brock Tuah (Ronald)." One name. ctay was there for all of it, co-managing the whole run, and still didn't make the plaque. Two years of co-ownership and the trophy engraver only had room for one of them. That's not a late arrival. That's worse: he was in the room the whole time and still got cropped out.

Two brains, one roster

Here's what the two of them have built in 2026, according to the actual numbers.

The QB room. The Co-Men spent a Round 3 pick (#36) on Justin Herbert. Herbert has 63.68 league points through Week 5. That's 12.7 a game, in a league with 6-point passing TDs. On Oct 16 they claimed Kirk Cousins off waivers for $0. Cousins had 116.56 points by then, nearly double Herbert. Two managers, three years of working together, a full draft prep, and the best quarterback they found was the one nobody wanted to pay a dollar for. Herbert is now a bench QB. So is the Round 16 pick Tyler Shough. Brock Tuah carries three quarterbacks, presumably one per manager plus a spare for the argument.

The Round 2 pick. J.K. Dobbins, #13 overall. 36.10 points in five weeks, 7.2 a game. He's on the bench. Two people agreed on that pick. Or one of them made it while the other was in the bathroom, and after three seasons together neither will say who.

The A.J. Brown experience. Round 10. Scored 5.60 points in Week 1, then 0.00, 0.00, 0.00, 0.00. On Oct 14 the Co-Men sent him to I Can't Hear Shit, and to get rid of him they had to attach a Round 3 pick and drop down to a Round 4 coming back (plus Rhamondre Stevenson). They paid a team to take their zero. Both managers had to sign off on that. Think about it.

The FLEX. Week 6's W/R/T slot: Jalen Coker, Round 4. Coker's last two weeks: 0.00 and 0.00. Two managers, one lineup, and the FLEX is a guy who has scored zero points in two straight weeks.

The medical wing. The IR has De'Von Achane and James Conner, who they picked up as a free agent while he was already on IR-R (Oct 14). Conner has 0.00 points this season. Two managers looked at the free-agent pool together and chose an injured running back. Achane has 24.60 points total and hasn't scored since Week 3. Also, Round 8 pick Tyreek Hill has scored 0.00 this season, which is now someone else's problem.

The 0.22 game

Week 3: Brock Tuah 157.36, The Man in Jahmyrror 157.58. That's a loss by 0.22 points, the closest game in the league this season.

Two managers. One lineup. And between the two of them, neither could find 0.23 points on a roster that includes Herbert, Dobbins and Shough. We have no idea what happened in the Co-Men group chat that Sunday. League lore says it was a 40-message argument about the FLEX that ended with both of them saying "whatever, your call." Lore is lore. The 0.22 is real.

Week 1 wasn't close: a 29.80-point loss to Bijan in the Nico Time, and their 136.16 ranked 7th that week. So their two losses are a blowout and a loss by less than a single reception. That's range.

Who gets the credit, who gets the blame?

This is the beauty of co-managing: you can never be wrong. Did JSN put up 139.46 league points, WR1 in the NFL? Obviously the brilliance of whoever's talking. Did Herbert lose a QB battle to a $0 waiver claim? That was the other guy's pick.

Except it doesn't work that way. They've been a duo since 2024, so the ledger is joint: the 2025 trophy is both of theirs, the empty 2024 shelf is both of theirs, and so is every zero on this page. Ronald and ctay get to split the parade and the A.J. Brown tax.

For the record, the draft board does show some fantastic value late: Brock Bowers (Rd 11), CeeDee Lamb (Rd 12), Jaxon Smith-Njigba (Rd 13), Chase Brown (Rd 14). JSN and Lamb are their two highest-scoring non-QBs, and they came off the board at picks 156 and 133. In a keeper league. So the Co-Men are elite at the part of the draft where nobody is paying attention, and spent their top picks on Parker Washington, Dobbins and Herbert. Two brains, and both of them were apparently saving the good thinking for Round 11.

Is it collusion if it's one team?

Technically no. But Brock Tuah is the only franchise in Echo Frezco where two people can draft, trade, argue, and still blame each other for a 0.22-point loss. Every other team has to live with its mistakes alone. The Co-Men have spent three seasons passing theirs back and forth.

The numbers that will be used against them

  • Record: 3-2, 4th.
  • Points for: 750.72, 3rd in the league, and they're still in 4th. Two managers and they can't convert points into standings.
  • Points against: 718.92, 3rd-highest in the league. The one honest excuse they have, and they have to share it.
  • FAB left: $84. Their best FAB move this year cost $0.
  • Moves: 13.

Week 6: live, lead, and a projection that should scare everyone

As of Sunday afternoon, Brock Tuah lead Dylan's King of Onlyfannins 73.40-62.68, with Yahoo projecting 158.89 to 111.94. So yes, the Co-Men will probably win this week. Dylan's team is 10th in points, so he's not exactly the 1985 Bears.

A backhanded hopeful note

Credit where it's due: there's a real chance this team is good. 750.72 points through five weeks is legit. JSN is a top WR, Lamb is WR2 in the NFL, Bowers is a top TE, and the $0 Cousins claim is outscoring everything they drafted at QB. If Brock Tuah repeats, both of them get to brag, and maybe this time Yahoo will spell out both names.

Just know that when it goes wrong, and it will, each one is already blaming the other. They've had since 2024 to practice.
