Week 7 Preview

Four NFL teams are on bye: Buffalo, Jacksonville, the Chargers and Washington. Projections and lineups are Yahoo's as of Tuesday afternoon (about 1:30 PM ET). Waivers haven't run yet, and every lineup can change before kickoff. Thursday night is NE @ CHI (8:15 PM ET).

Brock Tuah (proj 143.50) vs Backshots R Us! (proj 105.19)

4-2 vs 4-2. The defending champs (three straight wins) against Year 10 of the rebuild (coming off the top score of Week 6, 163.84). Yahoo has the Co-Men by 38.31. The Co-Men flipped their QBs this week: Tyler Shough, who scored 32.96 on the bench last week, starts (19.29), and Kirk Cousins (18.06) sits. Parker Washington and Justin Herbert are on bye, and Rhamondre Stevenson goes Thursday night. Adrian is starting Kenneth Walker III (18.62), and he still isn't for sale, so the Co-Men will just have to outscore him. Backshots' DEF slot is empty after the Panthers were cut, and James Cook, Jakobi Meyers and Keon Coleman are all on the bench on bye. Adrian has $0 of FAB to fix it with.

Bijan in the Nico Time (proj 148.62) vs The Josh Allen Red Rockets (proj 88.77)

The top projection of the week against the second-lowest. Yahoo has ABM by 59.85, the biggest gap of the week. The Red Rockets are named after Josh Allen, start Josh Allen, and Josh Allen is on bye. So is their kicker, Tyler Bass. Allen is the only QB on the roster, Romeo Doubs (IR) is in a starting WR slot, and Tyler Warren and Breece Hall are both listed Q. Antonio, two straight wins, has some shopping to do. Bijan has Lamar Jackson (Q) at QB with Aaron Rodgers (21.38) behind him, and needs more than 7.3 from Bijan Robinson this time.

BK The King (proj 130.18) vs The Man in Jahmyrror (proj 70.48)

Joshua's lineup, as of Tuesday afternoon: no QB, no DEF, two empty WR slots. Trevor Lawrence is on bye, and Cooks and Nailor were cut at 2:53 AM PT. Jahmyr Gibbs (25.30), Kyler Murray (22.44) and Ja'Marr Chase (19.74) are on his bench, back from bye and projected for 67.48 together. That's almost the whole team projection, sitting there. Yahoo has BK by 59.70 for now. With Murray at QB, Chase at WR and Gibbs in for Emanuel Wilson (7.24), Jahmyrror would project about 130.72, a hair over BK. That would still leave a WR slot and the DEF empty. BK lost three straight and gets Joe Burrow and Justin Jefferson back from bye. He also has Jordan Love (23.20) on his bench, projected ahead of Burrow (22.69). The King and his benches have a complicated relationship.

Love Thy Nabers (proj 141.98) vs Oldest Team on Earth (proj 128.39)

Two teams on losing streaks (MT L-2, Chris L-3). Yahoo has MT by 13.59. Jared Goff is back from bye (22.89) for MT, and Ladd McConkey (Q) sits on bye. Chris starts Dak Prescott and Derrick Henry (18.06), with Jordan Addison (Q) in a WR slot. Davante Adams (O), plus Jayden Daniels and Stefon Diggs (both on bye), are on the bench. Chris has made one move all season and still has $100 of FAB. MT has the opposite problem: he can't stop pitching trades. If you're Chris, don't answer your phone this week. It's MT, and it's a trade offer.

The King vs. the kingdom. King of Onlyfannins against OnlyFannins, and the actual OnlyFannins is 5-1 on a five-game win streak. Yahoo has OnlyFannins by 38.85. Dylan's lineup has an empty RB slot.
