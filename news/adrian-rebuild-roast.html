Year 10 of the Rebuild: Adrian Holds Press Conference, Confirms Kenneth Walker Is Still Not for Sale

According to league lore, and we stress lore, because nobody can find the receipts, Adrian has been "rebuilding" Backshots R Us! for roughly ten years. Ten years. Teams have folded, been renamed, and won titles in the time it has taken this franchise to lay its foundation. Historians disagree on when the rebuild started. Adrian disagrees that it's even a rebuild. He prefers the term "process."

So where does Year 10 of the process stand? We checked the actual 2026 numbers.

The state of the rebuild

  • Record: 3-2, 6th place, the last playoff spot, currently on an L-1 streak.
  • Points for: 604.24, 8th of 12. That's the lowest PF of anyone in a playoff spot, by a wide margin. The other two 3-2 teams ahead of him have scored 750.72 (Brock Tuah) and 728.02 (Love Thy Nabers). Backshots is winning with a points-against of 591.60 and a prayer.
  • Weekly scores: 100.44, 142.48, 122.44, 128.86, 110.02. Two losses, by 5.20 to Oldest Team on Earth and by 26.22 to King of Onlyfannins.
  • FAB remaining: $0. The whole budget is gone by Week 6. Only one other team (Goldie, 0-5) is also broke.
  • Moves: 36. That's the most in the league, and it isn't close. Second place is Goldie with 23. The league median is somewhere around "a normal person."

Thirty-six moves and zero FAB dollars, and the rebuild's centerpiece is still a guy he drafted in Round 13.

The draft: a foundation, partially

Backshots made 16 picks on draft night. Six are still on the roster: Kenneth Walker III, Bucky Irving, James Cook III, Kyle Pitts Sr., Tee Higgins and Adonai Mitchell, who is currently on IR with an O tag. The other ten have been sent back to the soil from which they came, including Round 6 pick Chris Bell, Round 9 picks Jalen McMillan and Tank Bigsby, and Round 15 pick Jordan Love, who now plays for BK The King and has 110.12 league points this season. For context, Backshots' current QB is Bryce Young, a free-agent pickup.

The Hunter Henry saga (a rebuild in miniature)

To truly understand the process, study one tight end:

  1. Draft night: Hunter Henry, Round 11, pick 130. A building block.
  2. Sometime later: Hunter Henry, gone. Released into the wild.
  3. Oct 17, 12:39 pm: Hunter Henry, added back as a free agent. Reunited.
  4. Oct 18, 6:50 am: Hunter Henry, dropped to waivers for Najee Harris. Eighteen hours.

Adrian drafted, dropped, re-signed and re-dropped the same tight end in one season. That's not roster churn. That's a relationship. The starting TE is now Kyle Pitts Sr., who has 26.90 league points through Week 5. That's TE31 in the NFL.

The press conference

Transcript from an emergency presser held at the Backshots R Us! training facility (Adrian's couch). Lightly edited for length.

Q: Adrian, Kenneth Walker has 110.10 points in four games. That's 27.5 a game, RB4 in the whole NFL in this league's scoring. Contenders are calling. Is he available?
ADRIAN: Kenneth Walker is not for sale.
Q: You're in Year 10 of a rebuild. Isn't selling a star for future assets the whole point of a rebuild?
ADRIAN: Kenneth Walker is not for sale.
Q: You have $0 in FAB and you've made 36 moves. You've clearly tried everything else. What would it take?
ADRIAN: He's not for sale. Next question.
Q: Would you take Brock Bowers?
ADRIAN: …No comment. (Long pause.) He's not for sale.
Q: Is anyone on this roster for sale?
ADRIAN: Hunter Henry. Again. Probably by Thursday.

Our reporters note that, in fairness, the man might be right this time. Walker is the best player he has, and our own Projected Keepers page has him as the Backshots keeper at RB, ahead of James Cook III (89.30) and Bucky Irving (78.70) in the FLEX. The math says keep him. The rebuild says sell him. Adrian says neither, because both answers involve admitting that the rebuild has a plan.

The Trade Block remains empty

As of publication, the official Trade Block has zero listings. Kenneth Walker is not on it. We asked if Adrian would like to post anyone. He asked if the form had a "not for sale" checkbox.

A hopeful note, sort of

Here's the thing nobody wants to say out loud: after five weeks, Year 10 of the process is sitting in a playoff spot. Backshots is 3-2 with the 8th-best offense, a broke FAB account and a tight end ranked 31st. That is either a miracle or the most efficient rebuild in league history. It might finally be paying off.

Just don't ask him to cash it in. Kenneth Walker is not for sale.
